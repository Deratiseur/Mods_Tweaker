Introduction

I created this mod because I wasn't happy with certain inconsistencies in other mods. It offers optional tweaks to install after the mods they affect.

The purpose of Mods Tweaker is not to fix bugs in other mods, but to offer changes that make their design more logical, consistent, or convenient to play. Every component is optional.

Companions & NPCs

Changes to characters, portraits, classes, and recruitment · 13 components

Horace — Give Horace the immunities of a skeleton.

Because he is a skeleton, yet lacks all the immunities normally associated with skeletons.

He gains 100% cold resistance, 50% resistance to slashing, piercing, and missile damage, and all undead immunities.

He also has 100% acid resistance. That isn't logical, but it was the author's choice, so I left it alone.

Horace — Move Horace to the Athkatla Graveyard.

Because Horace has only 150,000 XP when recruited, far too little for a party capable of reaching the crypt in Trademeet.

He is moved to the crypt beneath the central archway, the one the grave robbers emerge from at night. His dialogue still refers to the families of Trademeet.

Chiarra — Give Chiarra vampire immunities in her human form and set her race to “vampire.”

Because she is a vampire, yet lacks all the immunities normally associated with vampires, and her race is set to “human.”

She gains 50% resistance to cold and electricity, regeneration of 1 hit point every 2 seconds, and all undead immunities.

Her race becomes “vampire.” This is purely cosmetic unless you install the optional component of the “Valen NPC” mod.

Moddie — Give Moddie an appropriate portrait.

Because she is a fox (a wolf in the French version), yet her portrait shows a badger (and the same portrait is also used by the Coondred NPC mod).

The new portrait was kindly provided by scheele.

Moddie — Turn Moddie into an animal companion who follows you as a seventh party member.

Because she is an animal with animal dialogue, and equipping her with weapons, armor, and jewelry makes no sense.

Talk to her and give her a scratch to turn her into an animal companion. Talk to her again to bring her back into the party (to equip her, level her up, etc.). You can pet her or ask her to rejoin as often as you like.

Ninafer — Give Ninafer an alternative portrait.

Because Viconia looks good as a blonde, but having both characters in the party with essentially the same portrait feels wrong.

Portrait taken from the mod's official website. You may find it ugly, but it respects the author's choice.

Ninafer — Change Ninafer’s class to Wizard Slayer/Mage (she keeps her family equipment).

Because her dialogue revolves around hunting down evil mages.

Thael — Change Thael’s class to Swashbuckler/Mage.

Because his sword and mutant power make him suited to melee combat.

Tomoyo Sakagaki — Change Tomoyo Sakagaki’s class to Kensai.

Because in manga, girls who fight with katanas always wear school uniforms or kimonos, never armor.

Sub-Zero — Give Sub-Zero more reasonable ability scores.

Because he was created using Ctrl+8: he has 18(00) in every ability score.

Strength 16, Dexterity 18, Constitution 15, Intelligence 14, Wisdom 12, and Charisma 10. (Values agreed upon after discussion on the Couronne de Cuivre forum.)

Banana! — Give Banana! more reasonable ability scores.

Because he was created using Ctrl+8: he has 18(00) in every ability score.

Strength 16, Dexterity 18, Constitution 15, Intelligence 17, Wisdom 9, and Charisma 8.

Yikari — Give Yikari a little extra Charisma when he regains his speech.

Because he has a Charisma score of 4, and he doesn't deserve that.

His Charisma increases to 6 when he regains his speech.

Mawgul — Delay Mawgul’s appearance.

Because he starts with 2.5 million XP, far too much for a low-level party.

He will then appear in the same location, but only if the protagonist has at least 2 million XP.

Items, stores & quests

Rare items, additional merchants, and other adjustments · 9 components

Umbra of T.R.O.W — Major demons summoned in Umbra of T.R.O.W drop their hearts.

Balors, Ravagers, and Pit Fiends drop their hearts.

Because those hearts can be useful to smiths and other mods (such as Every Mods and Dogs).

Jamella's Diablo2 Item Store — Allow another merchant to sell items from Jamella's Diablo2 Item Store (without purchase limits).

Because offering 160 items for sale while letting you choose only one is a waste. What's more, the most powerful ones can only be purchased if the party includes at least two characters using kits from Diablo2 Kitpack.

The new merchant is near Watcher’s Keep. Prices are unchanged: 100,000 gold per item, with some costing 200,000 gold.

L'Interplan — Allow a merchant in ToB to sell every item obtainable from the Searcher’s Chest in L’Interplan.

Because there are more than 85 possible items, but you can only obtain one: what a waste.

The new merchant is in Amkethran, near the Astrolabe. If you install this component, the Searcher’s Chest is removed from L’Interplan’s stock. Since this merchant is only available in Amkethran, you will no longer be able to take advantage of the “special encounter” that occurs earlier in ToB through this method.

L'Interplan — A competing merchant sells the book “The Secrets of L’Interplan.”

Because some of this mod’s “secrets” are so well hidden that you have to wonder who, apart from the mod’s creator, could ever discover them.

The book is sold by Galoomp, the accountant directly opposite L’Interplan.

Mod d'anniversaire du Forum Sigil.cz — Make the amulet much easier to use.

Because using it requires opening the inventory, right-clicking the amulet, and choosing “Talk.” Not convenient at all.

Mod d'anniversaire du Forum Sigil.cz — Make the amulet truly unlimited.

Because once the summoned characters cast their offensive spells, they cannot be summoned again.

The Tortured Soul Quest — Give Baenwuld a portrait.

Because if you recruit him after freeing him, he either has no portrait or uses Minsc’s portrait.

The Tortured Soul Quest — Bring recruitable Baenwuld in line with his summonable version.

Because the recruitable version is weaker and less well equipped, which makes no sense given that they are the same character.

You will then be able to recruit the overpowered version (20 Strength, +2.5 attacks per round, +5 THAC0 bonus, +4 equipment).

The Tortured Soul Quest — Award XP for freeing Baenwuld from slavery.

Because finding a way to free him is a quest, after all.

Completing the quest awards 10,000 XP.

Kits

Ability and transformation adjustments · 2 components

Lion Warrior — Make the Lion Warrior kit’s shapeshifting semi-permanent (like shapeshifting altered by Tweak Anthology or SCS).

Because those mods do not account for shapeshifting abilities introduced by other mods.

Slime Mutant — Prevent the Slime Mutant kit from changing the character’s sex to androgynous.

Because it can interfere with romances.

Installation

First install the mods you want to tweak, then install Mods Tweaker. Select only the components you want in the WeiDU installer.

The original mods are required for their respective tweaks: Mods Tweaker does not include them or replace their installation.

For details about any component, see its description in the sections above.

Back to top

Compatibility

This mod is intended for installations that include the mods named in the component descriptions. Install Tweaker after those mods so that the resources to be modified are present.

Each component modifies specific resources or behaviors. Results may depend on the versions of the affected mods and on other tweaks installed; universal compatibility is not guaranteed.
